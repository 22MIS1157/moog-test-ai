## 3. Potential Failure Points

### 3.1 MOSFET Thermal Runaway (H-Bridge)
- **Location**: Q1–Q4, H-Bridge power stage
- **Root Cause**: Insufficient heatsinking, prolonged operation above 80% load with inadequate airflow. RDS(on) increases with temperature creating positive feedback loop.
- **Severity**: Critical (3/3)
- **Likelihood**: Medium (2/3)
- **RPN**: 6
- **Mitigation**: Add thermal pad to PCB, enforce thermal shutdown at 125°C, derate to 80% continuous current

### 3.2 Gate Driver Bootstrap Capacitor Degradation
- **Location**: C12 (100nF), U3 IR2110 bootstrap circuit
- **Root Cause**: Ceramic capacitor voltage coefficient — effective capacitance drops 40% at rated DC bias. After 5000+ hours, ESR increases.
- **Severity**: Critical (3/3)
- **Likelihood**: Medium (2/3)
- **RPN**: 6
- **Mitigation**: Use C0G/NP0 dielectric, oversize to 220nF, add bootstrap cap health monitoring

### 3.3 Current Sense Resistor Drift
- **Location**: R15 (10mΩ), current sense path
- **Root Cause**: Self-heating from I²R losses at 15A peak (2.25W). Metal element fatigue over thermal cycles.
- **Severity**: Critical (3/3)
- **Likelihood**: Low (1/3)
- **RPN**: 3
- **Mitigation**: Use 4-terminal Kelvin sense resistor, derate power by 50%, add redundant software overcurrent check

### 3.4 EtherCAT PHY Susceptibility to EMI
- **Location**: U7 KSZ8081, Ethernet magnetics
- **Root Cause**: High dV/dt switching noise from H-bridge couples into Ethernet traces via parasitic capacitance
- **Severity**: Major (2/3)
- **Likelihood**: Medium (2/3)
- **RPN**: 4
- **Mitigation**: Route Ethernet traces on opposite side from power stage, add common-mode choke, increase guard ring spacing

### 3.5 MCU Brownout from Power Supply Transients
- **Location**: U1 STM32F407, U2 voltage regulator
- **Root Cause**: Load step transients during PWM switching cause input voltage sag below UVLO threshold momentarily
- **Severity**: Critical (3/3)
- **Likelihood**: Low (1/3)
- **RPN**: 3
- **Mitigation**: Add bulk capacitance at regulator input (470μF), enable MCU brownout detection at 2.8V, implement soft-start sequence
